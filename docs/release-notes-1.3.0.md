# Create: Luviteium 1.3.0

## New content

- Two Curios-compatible charms: Nazar Boncuğu and Bayramın Bereketi.
- The charm group provides two available slots.
- Nazar Boncuğu grants Luck I and keeps non-boss hostile mobs from approaching within five blocks on any axis. A mob attacked by the player bypasses this protection for ten seconds.
- Bayramın Bereketi gives a 3% chance to receive 1–3 bonus produce when manually harvesting mature crops.
- Wooden, stone, iron, golden, diamond, netherite and Luvi paxels.
- Paxels combine pickaxe, axe and shovel functions; they do not provide hoe functionality.
- The netherite paxel is a Smithing Table upgrade of the diamond paxel.
- Paxel Head Mold and the 1008 mB Luvi casting flow.
- The charms are added at a low rate to dungeon, shipwreck supply and buried treasure chest loot.

## Technical note

Curios API 9.5.1+1.21.1 is declared as a compile-time and runtime dependency.
